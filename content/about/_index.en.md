I am Jia, a physics teacher.

I care not only about explaining a problem clearly, but also about how students build understanding: how they ask questions, construct models, evaluate evidence, and turn a vague idea into something they can explain.

This blog is my personal workbench. I use it to document:

- Notes from physics and education papers
- Teaching designs, PBL cases, and classroom observations
- Reflections on reading, writing, and personal growth

Writing is not a way to display answers for me. It is a way to continue thinking. Many things reveal whether I truly understand them only after I try to write them down.

If you are also interested in physics teaching, project-based learning, or how knowledge becomes understanding, you are welcome to get in touch through the links at the bottom of the page.
