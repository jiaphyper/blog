I realized that I had read many books, yet I could explain fewer and fewer of them clearly.

It was not a problem with memory. I simply had not stopped to think while reading. The words passed through my eyes, left a vague impression, and then disappeared.

Writing things down changed that. The moment you try to put an idea into words, you discover whether you actually understand it.

$$\text{Understanding} \neq \text{Having read}$$

That is why I started this blog: not primarily to be read by others, but to make myself think carefully.
